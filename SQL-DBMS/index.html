DBMS & SQL Interview Bible

A nine-part, exhaustive prep series for DBMS & SQL rounds at SDE2-level product-company interviews. PostgreSQL examples throughout. Every part ends with a "popular interview questions" section, and Part I is a dedicated top-20 drill.

A

Foundations

DBMS core concepts & RDBMS vs NoSQL, ER modeling & schema design, keys & constraints.

B

Normalization & Schema Design

Insert/update/delete anomalies explained concretely, 1NF→BCNF→4NF with the anomaly-to-fix mapping, and when to deliberately denormalize.

C

SQL Mastery

Query execution order, joins & their common bugs, subqueries vs CTEs, window functions, aggregate patterns, set ops, and a 9-problem curated real-interview SQL bank.

D

Transactions & Concurrency

ACID with a worked example, isolation levels & every anomaly they trade off, locking/deadlocks/2PL vs MVCC, savepoints, 2PC vs Sagas.

E

Storage, Indexing & Performance

Index types & when they hurt, composite/covering indexes, reading an EXPLAIN plan, storage engine internals, views vs materialized views vs procedures vs triggers.

F

Distributed Data

Replication & CAP in practice, sharding strategies, SQL vs NoSQL decision framework, and three fully worked schema-design case studies (Uber, Instagram-style feed, ticket checkout).

G

Security & Ops

SQL injection prevention & access control, zero-downtime migrations (expand-contract), and the production failure patterns everyone eventually hits.

H

Interview Simulation

Rapid-fire cheat sheet, company-pattern notes, and a mock interview drilling script rehearsing "why," "what if," and "how would you debug" follow-ups.

I

Top SQL Interview Questions

A one-hour-before-the-interview drill: 20 concept questions plus 20 pure "write a query to..." implementation problems (solved), cross-referenced against recent candidate-reported patterns.

Eight parts, one bible. Read in order, or jump straight to what you need.
